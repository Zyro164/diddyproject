Verdict

This is an account-spam and account-farming toolkit. It does not patch Free Fire, hook a process, or touch game memory. It logs into thousands of accounts and abuses Garena’s own OAuth and social endpoints to flood one target UID with friend requests and duo/squad invites. It can also mint new guest accounts and upload them to a Telegram bot.

What it does

  • Loads a bundled JSON dump of pre-authenticated accounts.
  • Re-logs each account through Garena MSDK to obtain a session.
  • Sends friend requests or raw-TCP duo/squad invites to a target.
  • Registers fresh guest accounts and saves them to external storage.
  • Uploads generated accounts through a Telegram bot.
  • Floats an overlay so it can run while the game is open.

What it is not

  • No native .so libraries, no Frida, no injection.
  • No memory editing, aimbot, ESP, or game-state modification.
  • No original public source repository; this is a private Kotlin build.
  • No proof the embedded accounts belong to the app’s users — they look machine-generated.
02

Headline findings

Six facts explain almost everything the app does.

01 Critical

Live credential dump in assets

6,190 records with passwords and signed JWTs are extractable by anyone who has the APK. The dump is in assets/accounts1.json.

02 Critical

Credentials sent to a non-Garena host

The token grant posts UID and password to ffmconnect.ppmainecoonghj.com, not a Garena-owned name. Treat it as a harvesting risk.

03 High

Hardcoded operator material

Static MSDK client secret, AES key/IV, HMAC key, and a reseller bearer JWT are compiled in and burned.

04 High

Account farm with exfiltration

New accounts are registered, written to GenAccount.txt, and uploaded to a Telegram bot.

05 Medium

Overlay plus all-files access

Draws over other apps, reads the clipboard, and writes to shared storage from a foreground service.

06 Medium

Self-signed, v2-only, cleartext allowed

No v1 signature, no pinning, usesCleartextTraffic=true, and an exported launcher activity.

Handling the sample

Sensitive material

The records are live authentication material. Do not import, replay or test them. Possession of the APK is possession of the dump; treat every embedded account as compromised and report the artifact rather than using it.

This site publishes the structure, not the credentials. See Detection & response for indicators and containment steps, and Method for the redaction policy.
